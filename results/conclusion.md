On Apple M2 using neon and 1 CPU worker, the FP32 operator timings with Mamba-3 checkpoint-layer fixtures were Mamba-3 SISO 75.6 ± 0.8 µs (0.85 FLOPs/byte), Mamba-3 MIMO-4 174.7 ± 1.5 µs (2.69 FLOPs/byte), Mamba-2 41.4 ± 1.0 µs (0.62 FLOPs/byte) per step (mean ± sample SD). Rank-4 MIMO was 131.0% slower than SISO by mean latency, with paired changes from +128.8% to +132.2% across 3 repeats. All three used a 768 KiB main recurrent matrix. In the separate single-worker profile, state update and readout was the largest timed MIMO-4 phase at 122.9 µs per step. The intensity uses modeled arithmetic FLOPs and minimum logical FP32 traffic. Actual DRAM traffic depends on cache residency. The system recorded swap-ins, with zero major page faults during all native timed loops.
